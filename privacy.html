Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Augusto MrGhossty ("the app") is used only by MrGhossty for his own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Basic account info (account name and ID), to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos, used only to post MrGhossty's own videos as private posts or drafts.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data from other people.
  • It does not sell or share any data.
  • It does not read messages, followers or other private content.

Storage

Access tokens are kept in encrypted secret storage and are never published. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok or Instagram app settings.
